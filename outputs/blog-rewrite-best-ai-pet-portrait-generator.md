## What Is an AI Pet Portrait Generator?

An AI pet portrait generator is a tool that takes an ordinary photo of your pet and uses generative AI models to repaint it in an art style — like Pixar 3D, classical oil painting, or watercolor. Unlike asking a human artist (which costs $80–$300 and takes 1–2 weeks), AI generators produce a finished portrait in under a minute, for a fraction of the price.

The three things that separate a good AI pet portrait generator from a bad one:

1. **It keeps your pet's actual features** — same breed, fur color, ear shape, even the look in the eyes. Bad generators turn every golden retriever into a generic cartoon dog.
2. **It nails the style** — a royal portrait should look like an 18th-century oil painting, not a filter.
3. **It's fast and cheap enough that you can iterate** — generate 3 styles, pick the best, then print.

PixPaw is built around all three.
