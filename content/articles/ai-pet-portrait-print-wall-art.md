## Path A: at-home or local print shop

Many people stop at a digital file and print on their own timeline.

1. **Download** the clean keeper after you spend credits.  
2. **Size for the frame** you already own (or a cheap ready-made frame from a craft store). Start modest — a desk print or small wall frame — then reprint larger later if you love it.  
3. **Choose paper lightly.** Matte photo paper often reads calmer on a wall than high-gloss glare under lamps.  
4. **Local shop option.** Drop the file at a neighborhood print counter if you want better paper without buying a canvas SKU online.

Credits that **never expire** help here: generate now, print when the frame arrives or when the holiday rush ends. The generator itself is not a shipping waitlist.

## Framing and size mindset

You do not have to commit to a huge statement piece on the first try.

- **Start modest.** A small frame or desk print teaches you how the style reads in real light.  
- **Reprint later.** Save your clean download so you can print it again. Check how the file looks at the new size before ordering a larger print.  
- **Match the room.** Try Magazine Chic, Bordeaux Muse, or Emerald Muse and compare the colors with your room and frame. Pick what still looks like *your* pet at arm’s length.

The credit pack is inventory for files, not a subscription waitlist for wall art.

## When to wait before you print

Pause the printer (or the canvas order) if:

- The **face is blurry** or cropped hard at the ear — swap the photo first.  
- The watermarked preview still looks “off” — fix style or upload before buying more credits.  
- You are in a **holiday shipping rush** and need a physical object tomorrow — download the digital file now, ship canvas later, or print locally same-day if a shop is open.

Digital-first is the flexible move: lock the likeness, then decide home print vs shop canvas on your calendar.
