## Photo that works for holiday looks

You do not need a studio. A few details make the face easier to work with:

- **Face clear.** Eyes and nose in frame. If a Santa hat or scarf sits low, tip it up or shoot slightly above so the eyes stay readable.
- **Soft window light.** Daylight beside a tree beats harsh flash bounce off ornaments. Soft light keeps markings readable in the preview.
- **Lights and tree in the background.** Bokeh and garlands behind or beside the pet are fine. Keep strings of lights and baubles clear of the eyes and muzzle.
- **One calm still beats a blurry chase.** Wait for a half-second of stillness or use a treat to catch their attention before taking the shot.

If the first upload looks soft or cropped at the ear, swap the photo before you spend credits. A calm photo taken before the tree goes up can work well once you add the holiday style.
