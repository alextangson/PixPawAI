## Photo basics for a gift-worthy keep

You do not need a studio. A phone photo is enough when:

- The **face is visible** (eyes and nose in frame, not cropped at the ear).
- Lighting is ordinary daylight or a bright indoor lamp — avoid heavy motion blur.
- One clear pet beats a busy group shot for a first gift try.

If the preview looks “off,” swap the photo before you buy more credits. Fixing the upload is cheaper than repeating a bad crop.
