# AI Pet Portrait Credits: Personal Use vs Commercial

You bought credits for a keep — a phone photo of your dog or cat turned into a styled portrait you want to download. Then a different question shows up: *can I sell this?* That is not the same as “do I like this file?”

On [PixPawAI](https://pixpawai.com/en/), credit packs are **one-time purchases**. Credits **never expire**. They are **not a subscription**. Starter is **$4.99 / 15 credits** on [pricing](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/) — the **personal-use** lane for home keeps, family gifts, and quiet social sharing. If your job sounds like selling merch, running ads, or product art, read live [pricing](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/), [Terms](https://pixpawai.com/en/terms/), and [contact support](https://pixpawai.com/en/contact/) (`support@pixpawai.com`) when unsure.

Start with what you want to do with the portrait: a personal memorial or home keepsake, a commercial social-media post, or merchandise and advertising.

## What “personal use” means here

A personal keepsake might be wallpaper, a memorial portrait for your home, a file for family, or a personal post on your own feed.

That is the Starter lane. On PixPawAI, **Starter Pack = personal use** (no watermark on owned keepers). The separate **HD Digital Portrait** unlock also lists a **personal print license** for one clean file.

Personal use is *not* “every download is shop-ready.” Selling shirts, running paid ads, or licensing to a client is a commercial question — see the next section, then Pricing / Terms / Contact.

## What the commercial-use labels mean on PixPawAI

Choose a pack by both the number of credits you need and its usage label.

The short usage labels in [Terms §5.2](https://pixpawai.com/en/terms/) are:

- **Starter Pack** — personal use  
- **Pro Bundle** — social media commercial use (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)  
- **Master Plan** (sold as a **one-time credit pack**, despite the “Plan” name) — full commercial use license (including merchandise, advertising)

For a commercial social-media post, look at Pro’s label; for merchandise or advertising, look at Master’s label. Read the full [Terms](https://pixpawai.com/en/terms/), check current packs on [Pricing](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/), and [contact support](https://pixpawai.com/en/contact/) at **support@pixpawai.com** if you are unsure whether your intended use is covered.

## Same credits workflow either way

Personal or commercial intent does not change the generation loop:

1. Upload a clear pet photo on [PixPawAI](https://pixpawai.com/en/)  
2. Pick a live style card  
3. Use the **Free** path — **1–2 watermarked tries** to preview the result — to judge likeness  
4. When you want clean, watermark-free keepers, buy a **credit pack** (or unlock a single HD file if that fits)

Each portrait spends credits from the pack you own. The workflow is the same; the **usage lane** on the pack is what differs. Secure checkout. One-time purchases. No subscription.

## FAQ

**Is Starter a subscription?**  
No. Starter is a **one-time** credit pack — **$4.99 / 15 credits**. Pricing states one-time purchases and no subscription. Credits never expire.

**Do unused credits expire?**  
No. Purchased generation credits remain until used.

**Does personal use cover a birthday gift file for family?**  
Usually yes for a personal keep you give to family — that sits in the **personal-use** lane (Starter). It is not the same as selling the design as a product. If the “gift” is actually merchandise for sale, read Pro/Master on pricing and contact support.

**Do I need Master to post one portrait on my own Instagram?**  
Not automatically. A quiet personal share of your own pet keep is usually the Starter personal lane. If the post is **commercial** social use (promoting a business, selling with the image, ad creative), check Pro / Master labels on [Terms](https://pixpawai.com/en/terms/) / [pricing](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/) and **contact support** if unsure.
