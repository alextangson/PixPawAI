## What “free” usually means in this category

Four patterns show up again and again. None of them are “unlimited, print-ready, forever.”

**A watermarked preview.** You generate something, then a logo or a translucent overlay sits on the file. You can see the idea. You cannot honestly post it as a finished portrait or send it to a printer.

**A credit-card wall after one look.** The site lets you start. The download, the high-resolution export, or the “remove watermark” button waits behind a card form.

**A weekly or monthly app trial.** The first days feel free. Then a recurring charge starts, or the gallery locks until you subscribe. That can make sense if you generate every week. It is a poor fit if you wanted two birthday cards and then nothing until December.

**A generic “unlimited” image tool.** Some results are just an all-purpose image generator with the word “pet” in the headline. They are not a pet-portrait checkout with a face-first upload and a named style list. Treat those as a different product, not a free version of the same thing.

None of those patterns are ranked here, and this page does not paste other apps’ prices. If you want a billing-model map — credit pack vs pay-per-file vs subscription — that is a different article. This one stays on **what “free” is allowed to mean**.
