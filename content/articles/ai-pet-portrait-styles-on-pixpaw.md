# AI Pet Portrait Styles on PixPaw: What You Can Actually Pick

**AI pet portrait styles** on [PixPawAI](https://pixpawai.com/en/) are named buttons on the site — not a vague "type any movie franchise" prompt box. You upload a face-clear pet photo, pick a live style card, and generate. Free means **1–2 watermarked tries**. Watermark-free files start at [Starter $4.99 / 15 credits](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/) — one-time, credits never expire, not a subscription.

This page lists what you can actually click today. It is not a ranked tool roundup, and it is not a second royal-costume encyclopedia.

## Names that show up in FAQ — not a second engine

The homepage FAQ says you can choose from **8+** styles and names **Cinematic Style**, **Merry Christmas**, **Royal Portrait**, and **Retro Pop Art** among others. Treat those as **on-site labels**, not proof of separate AI engines.

For a longer browse of royal / costume looks, use the live [royal pet portrait styles](https://pixpawai.com/en/blog/10-royal-pet-portrait-styles-your-dog-or-cat-deserves/) post. This article will not rewrite that list.

## What Free vs Starter unlocks (style counts)

From the [pricing page](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/) on write day:

- **Free Trial** — **3 basic styles**, watermarked, 1–2 generations
- **Starter** — **8 style options**, no watermarks, **$4.99 / 15 credits**

Higher packs list more styles on the same pricing page. This post does not rebuild that matrix. Open pricing for the current cards, and trust what the generate UI shows for your pack.

## What we do *not* sell as a named style

**Pixar, Ghibli, and Disney** are not named home-grid styles. A footer link labeled "Cartoon Style Dogs" that points at a gallery filter is **not** the same as a Pixar product button — do not shop for "Pixar mode" here.

Also not claimed:

- Unlimited Midjourney-style prompts as the catalog
- A dog-only or cat-only model switch
- "Any style you imagine" as a guaranteed SKU

Pick a live card. Generate. Decide if you want credits.

## How to choose fast

- **Holiday card** → Merry Christmas
- **Fashion still** → Magazine Chic, Bordeaux Muse, or Emerald Muse
- **Bold graphic** → Wes Anderson Pop or Retro Pop Art
- **Casual gift** → Smart Casual or Music Lover

Then use a **face-visible** photo (phone is fine), run a watermarked try, and buy Starter if you want clean keepers.

## FAQ

**Do you have a Pixar style?**\
No named Pixar product on the home style grid. Pick a live card such as Retro Pop Art or Magazine Chic instead.

**How many styles do I get on Starter?**\
Pricing lists **8 style options** on Starter (confirm on the live pricing page when you buy). Free Trial lists **3 basic styles**.

**Can I type any custom prompt?**\
The FAQ mentions optional custom prompts (for example a hat or a beach). Your base pick is still a **named on-site style**.

**Is this a subscription?**\
No. Credit packs are one-time. Credits never expire.
