## Three quick filters before you tap

Run these in order. They take under a minute.

1. **Occasion** — Everyday keep, holiday card, party energy, or a fashion-forward still for the desk or feed?
2. **Vibe** — Quiet and elegant, casual and friendly, or bold and shareable?
3. **Photo readiness** — Is the face clear? Soft light? Or is the background busy, the room dark, or accessories competing with the eyes?

If the face is clear, choose a card that matches your occasion and vibe. If clutter competes with the face, crop first; if the face is dark or blurred, choose a clearer photo before generating. Then try a quieter look or a bolder card according to the mood you want.

### One short photo tip (not a full photo guide)

Phone photos are fine. Aim for a visible face (eyes and nose in frame), soft daylight or a bright lamp, and as little motion blur as you can. If the preview looks wrong, fix the upload before you burn more credits.

## When to try a quieter card first

If you want a calmer result, consider Smart Casual, Bordeaux Muse, Emerald Muse, or Magazine Chic before Retro Pop Art or Wes Anderson Pop. Before tapping, check whether:

- The **background is busy** (toys, people, clutter fighting the silhouette)
- The **room is dark** or the face is half in shadow
- **Accessories or costumes** already crowd the frame and you mainly want the pet’s face to stay readable

Crop out distractions or choose a better-lit photo first; a quieter card cannot recover a face hidden by blur or shadow. After the preview looks like *your* pet, you can try a bold card using the same clear crop. Check the credit cost in the generate UI before each paid generation.
