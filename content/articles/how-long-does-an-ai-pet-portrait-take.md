# How Long Does an AI Pet Portrait Take?

You already did the hard part — you found a photo where the face is visible. Now the page is thinking, and every second feels longer than a salon wait. So: **how long does an AI pet portrait take** on [PixPawAI](https://pixpawai.com/en/)?

On the live site FAQ, most portraits are ready in about **20–40 seconds**. More complex styles may take up to about **60 seconds**. That is the public clock we use here. We are not quoting internal lab averages, and we are not promising a fixed sub-15-second SLA.

If you want watermark-free keepers after the try, [Starter is $4.99 / 15 credits](https://pixpawai.com/en/pricing/) — one-time, credits never expire, not a subscription.

## What “ready in ~30 seconds” actually means

The homepage also markets **Ready in 30 Seconds** as a friendly round number. Treat it as the same story as the FAQ band: a typical job finishes in under a minute when the upload is clear and the queue is normal.

“Ready” usually means you can **preview** the styled portrait. Watermarked free tries and paid keepers are different checkout moments — the clock above is about generation, not about shipping a canvas.

## What happens while you wait

In plain language:

1. The photo is checked for a usable face.
2. Your chosen **named style** is applied (the live style cards on the homepage — not a free-text movie franchise box).
3. A preview returns so you can keep, retry, or buy credits.

You do not need design software. You do need a face that is not cropped off the frame.

## What makes a job feel slow

A few ordinary reasons — none of them require a technical error code:

- **Blurry or side-on faces.** The model spends the same wall-clock time and still returns something you will reject. Fix the photo first; see the live [blurry pet photo guide](https://pixpawai.com/en/blog/blurry-pet-photo-to-stunning-ai-art-guide/).
- **Busy moments.** Like any cloud tool, peak traffic can stretch a run toward the high end of the FAQ band.
- **A safety stop.** Sometimes a generation is blocked before a finished portrait appears. That is different from “I don’t like the style.” Retry with a clearer pet-face crop; details live on the [FAQ](https://pixpawai.com/en/faq/).

If a run finishes but you dislike the look, use the satisfaction options already described on the FAQ (credit help / try another style). Do not confuse a finished preview you reject with a blocked job.
