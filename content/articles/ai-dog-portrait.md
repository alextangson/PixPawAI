## Photo tips that matter for dogs (short)

Phone photos are fine. Filters are not. The homepage is blunt: good light, a visible face, and a file that is not a blur.

- **Face and eyes readable** — looking toward the camera beats a mid-zoom sniff of the carpet.
- **Window light in front** — backlight turns a black or chocolate coat into a silhouette.
- **Ears and snout in the frame** — especially floppy ears and long muzzles; do not crop them off.
- **One main dog** — a second head in the corner steals the composition.
- **Long fur** — part the hair so the eyes and nose are not buried.

That is enough for this species page. If the file is already a lost cause, use the [blurry pet photo guide](https://pixpawai.com/en/blog/blurry-pet-photo-to-stunning-ai-art-guide/) rather than repeating a full shoot checklist here.
